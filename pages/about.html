Our Story

From a Single Shop to London's Pet Sanctuary

It all began in 1987 with a simple idea: pets deserve the same quality of life we give ourselves. Our founders opened a modest supply shop with a radical promise — premium products, expert care, and warmth in everything we do.

Today, Tails & Tags is more than a store. It is a community of devoted pet parents, a place where every tail wags a little harder. We curate the world's finest brands, craft signature grooming rituals, and treat every companion like family.
