Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Do I need an appointment for grooming?

Yes, grooming sessions are by appointment. You can book through this form or call us at 1-800-PET-LOVE, and we'll find a slot that suits you.

Can I visit the store with my pet?

Absolutely! We're a pet-friendly store. Well-behaved pets on a leash (or in a carrier) are always welcome to browse with you.

How fast do you reply to messages?

We reply to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days. For urgent matters, calling us directly is always the fastest option.

Do you offer boarding services?

Yes, we offer short and long-term boarding with daily walks, playtime, and vet supervision. Select "Boarding Booking" in the form above for details.
